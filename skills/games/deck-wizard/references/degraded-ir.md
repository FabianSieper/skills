# Missing structural card IR

Read when a toolkit warning mentions a missing crosswalk/Phase card IR, when a
known commander mechanic has no signals, or when all role/engine flags are zero.
Those fields are unavailable evidence, not a clean structural verdict.

1. Record the affected command and warning in the run.
2. Keep reliable data: exact inventory, complete Oracle text, dated legality,
   prices and independently computed physical quantities.
3. Complete the small per-card evaluations. `aggregate` counts their verified roles
   and source classifications deterministically. Check a sample of each role
   against the underlying paragraphs, and inspect every sole critical provider.
4. Count colored sources from actual abilities, basic types and real fetch targets;
   separate conditional, tapped and unrestricted early sources. MDFCs are choices.
5. Use Oracle searches to locate possibilities, then evaluate every match. Absence
   of a string such as “destroy target” does not prove absence of interaction.
   Fight, sacrifice, damage, bounce, counters and other effects have different text.
6. Record what remains unmeasured and how it limits confidence. Finish rule-critical
   verification before recommending the affected interaction.

Never impose a universal land band, infer a missing late game from a low printed
curve, or rank cards solely by graph degree. Judge actual mana usage, alternate
costs, activated sinks, role needs and the user plan.
